CYBER UPGRADE - 9-MONTH MASTER COURSE CATALOG (DRAFT 01)
36 weeks | 144 classes | Tuesday-Friday 5h/day | 20h/week | 720 hours
Status: Course blueprint only. Full original textbooks, lab manuals, exercises, quizzes and answer explanations will be created separately after approval.
Student does not need to research each topic independently: each finished weekly learning packet must contain explanation, examples, guided labs, troubleshooting, original questions, explained answers, and assignments.
Class day pattern: Tuesday lesson; Wednesday technical lab; Thursday applied lab/project; Friday leadership translation, quiz and deliverable.
Chegg Skills/Thinkful progression is an organizational reference, not copied proprietary content. Exact screenshot module-by-module match pending verification.

Ingest synthetic telemetry; at least five investigated alerts; search/detection logic; false-positive tuning; SOC metrics and shift handoff.

Governance charter, risk register, staffing and RACI, 12-month roadmap, policies, budget, metrics and board presentation.
